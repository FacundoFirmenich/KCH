# Contributing to KCH

Contributions should target a versioned successor and preserve stable predecessors, adverse results and rollback paths.

1. Open a focused branch.
2. State the objective, jurisdiction, evidence boundary and affected host.
3. Add or update a functional test.
4. Do not transfer authority from a local test to another host or domain.
5. Do not rewrite failed gates; supersede them with a new dated result.
6. Keep PHL training disabled unless a separately governed protocol and genuine user feedback authorize it.
7. Never include secrets, account material or machine-specific credentials.

Pull requests should explain what became operational, what remains unvalidated and how to recover the predecessor.
