# Integrated framework state — BIFM / SSEC / CTEC

## 1. Ontological object classes

### SSEC

A statistical-computational software system is the executable learned or hybrid artifact:

- architecture;
- parameters and state;
- data-dependent estimation;
- predictive or scoring law;
- decision/decoding rule;
- memory and tools available at inference;
- software execution environment.

### CTEC

A technical-statistical-computational configuration contains the SSEC plus:

- hardware and sensors;
- energy and material infrastructure;
- networks and external services;
- human and organizational operators;
- owners and institutions;
- permissions and policies;
- authority leases;
- effectors and execution receipts.

The SSEC can produce a candidate. The CTEC can produce a world effect.

## 2. Biological boundary

Artificial systems may implement functions inspired by biological organization. Functional analogy does not establish:

- homologous physical mechanism;
- metabolism or autopoiesis;
- organismic development;
- interoceptive/affective embodiment;
- phenomenal consciousness;
- normative or political authority.

Every biological or phenomenal attribution requires a dedicated identifying bridge. The framework does not assert that every future artificial system must fail every biological or consciousness criterion; it blocks unsupported inheritance from behavior.

## 4. Identifiability core

For `O : M -> Y` and `q : M -> Q`, `q` is identifiable from `O` iff:

`O(m1)=O(m2) => q(m1)=q(m2)`.

Equivalent form:

`q = g o O` on the admissible class.

Positive certificate: factor map.

Negative certificate: collision witness.

Valid terminal states include `NOT_IDENTIFIABLE`, `INSUFFICIENT_POWER`, `PRACTICALLY_UNSTABLE` and `OUT_OF_SCOPE`.

## 5. Observation lattice

No untyped object called “the behavior” is assumed. Observations form a partial order:

- coarse output decision;
- ranking / score-vector signature;
- quantized predictive distribution;
- vocabulary-level signature;
- internal activation or logit telemetry;
- intervention receipt and provenance.

Finer observations contract the invisible intervention set. More stimuli contract it when observations are nested.

## 6. Intervention quotient

The full chain is:

`control command --R_A--> realized internal transition --O--> observation`.

Architecture may collapse different commands before observation. Observation may collapse different realized transitions afterward.

Raw operator names or strengths are not comparable causal units. Commands must be quotient by architecture-relative realized-transition equivalence or accompanied by realized-effect metrics.

## 10. ECH/HCS role

ECH/HCS governs the statistical composition:

- calibration;
- dependence and effective evidence;
- jurisdiction discovery;
- adaptive resource allocation;
- abstention;
- cost and risk;
- promotion gates.

More agents or probes do not automatically add evidence. Dependence and conditional value must be estimated.

## 11. KCH role

KCH separates:

`capability != support != permission != authority != execution != training`.

A model output cannot construct its own authority lease. A statistically preferred action remains a recommendation until externally authorized. Runtime effects require receipts and revocation/override paths.

## 12. Genealogy and local super-capability

A system can be genealologically derived from human technoculture and locally outperform humans in a task without contradiction:

`A triangleleft_g H` and `A >_D H`.

Performance order, causal dominance, genealogy, biological organization and authority are distinct partial orders.

Loss of control can occur without consciousness or biological superiority when a CTEC combines access, speed, opacity, replication and delegated authority.

## 13. Scientific conclusion currently supported

Finite behavioral equality under a declared observation operator can fail to identify a known, materially different internal intervention state. Refining the observation or adding probes can contract or split the equivalence class. Therefore behavioral resemblance alone does not license mechanistic, biological, phenomenal, normative or authority equivalence.

## 14. Claims not supported

The programme does not establish:

- that artificial systems can never understand;
- that artificial consciousness is impossible;
- that all historical AI is statistical;
- that mechanism is never inferable from behavior;
- that humans globally dominate every artifact;
- that current posterior estimates generalize to all prompts or models;
- that capability grants authority.
