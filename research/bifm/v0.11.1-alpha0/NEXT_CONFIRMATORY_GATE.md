# Future-only successor gate after v0.11.1

No successor experiment is selected until the frozen v0.11.1 exact tables and posterior/EIG receipts exist.

## Candidate dimensions

### Model family

- third open-weight transformer family;
- replication of Qwen or OLMo under a new revision is secondary to family diversity.

### Domain

- non-ARC real prompts;
- multilingual native-language suite from the TALM/TALON preregistration is preferred because it supplies segment and language jurisdictions.

### Intervention family

- frozen TALM/TALON pre-softmax operator;
- activation patching or learned direction;
- MLP scaling/ablation retained only as comparators.

### Observation family

- generated-output top-1 / exact text;
- rank and token-distribution telemetry;
- local structural and semantic descriptors;
- full operator-command and receipt telemetry.

## Selection rule

The next gate must combine:

1. at least one high-EIG v0.11 cell;
2. at least one low-EIG control cell;
3. a new model or domain jurisdiction;
4. a second intervention family;
5. frozen baseline and explicit rivals;
6. no use of confirmatory outcomes to choose the candidate.

## TALM/TALON future-only roles

The first bridge campaign must evaluate separately:

- collision-probe capability;
- separator capability;
- sensor utility for future routing;
- actuator utility for local task performance.

Z_xpl may provide post-hoc cartography but cannot select or certify the confirmatory candidate.

## Promotion criteria

- `REPRODUCED_SOURCE`: exact operator source/configuration recovered and hashed;
- `REALIZATION_QUOTIENTED`: duplicate or near-duplicate commands collapsed by realized telemetry;
- `COLLISION_CERTIFIED`: coarse observation equality with nontrivial realized transition;
- `SEPARATOR_CERTIFIED`: frozen finer probe distinguishes the collision;
- `SENSOR_VALIDATED`: prospective routing/abstention gain;
- `ACTUATOR_VALIDATED`: prospective local utility against explicit rivals;
- `AUTHORIZED`: external KCH lease.

A candidate can pass sensor validation while failing actuator validation. Neither implies authority.
