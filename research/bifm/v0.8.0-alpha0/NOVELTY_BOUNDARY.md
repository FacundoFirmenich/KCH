# Novelty boundary for the v0.8 mechanistic track

The mechanistic experiment is not claimed as the first demonstration that activation/steering interventions can be non-identifiable from behavior.

A direct 2026 antecedent is:

- Sohan Venkatesh and Ashish Mahendran Kurapath, *On the Non-Identifiability of Steering Vectors in Large Language Models*, arXiv:2602.06801v4. The paper studies equivalence classes of behaviorally indistinguishable steering interventions and estimates null-space structure empirically.

Additional adjacent work treats activation steering as a causal mediation problem and performs targeted hidden-state interventions.

The residual BIFM contribution targeted by v0.8 is narrower and compositional:

1. the empirical intervention is interpreted through a prior, proof-carrying claim-identifiability calculus;
2. the target property is explicitly the intervention state, not a vague notion of "mechanism";
3. the observation operator is frozen and finite;
4. positive collision witnesses and held-out counterfactual probes are reported separately;
5. pre-softmax and internal interventions are separated;
6. the result is integrated with SSEC/CTEC typing and later KCH authority claims;
7. no biological, phenomenal or semantic conclusion is inherited from the behavioral result.

Accordingly, v0.8 is a bridge experiment validating the general BIFM calculus on a real SSEC, not a priority claim over steering-vector non-identifiability.
