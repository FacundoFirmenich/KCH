# Novelty and adjacency boundary

BIFM v0.9 does not claim priority for the general fact that steering or activation interventions may be non-identifiable from behavior.

Direct prior work includes:

- Venkatesh & Kurapath (2026), *On the Identifiability of Steering Vectors in Large Language Models*, which proves and empirically studies equivalence classes of behaviorally indistinguishable steering interventions and recovery under added assumptions.

The residual contribution targeted by v0.9 is the composition of:

1. a general proof-carrying claim-identifiability calculus;
2. explicit observation/property typing;
3. exact collision certificates;
4. permutation-controlled real-data observation operators;
5. inter-model replication as a separately gated result;
6. an empirical layer-by-sample invisibility frontier;
7. probe-based recovery and future integration with SSEC/CTEC and KCH authority boundaries.

Any claim of broader novelty remains provisional pending a systematic literature review.
