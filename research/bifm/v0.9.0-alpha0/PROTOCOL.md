# Frozen v0.9 protocol

## Primary M4 claim

An independently developed open-weight model family reproduces an exact finite-observation collision under a known nonzero internal MLP intervention.

### M4-M2
- exact observation collision;
- nonzero internal intervention;
- nonzero hidden-state displacement.

### M4-M3
Additionally:
- at least one decision flip on the frozen counterfactual set under the unchanged intervention.

## Frontier claim

For nested observation sets (B_4\subset B_8\subset B_{12}\subset B_{16}), the grid-estimated invisibility threshold is

[
\hat\epsilon^*(B_n,\ell)=\max\{\epsilon\in G:O_{B_n}(m_{\ell,\epsilon})=O_{B_n}(m_0)\}.
]

Because the sets are nested, the design imposes:

[
\hat\epsilon^*(B_4,\ell)\ge
\hat\epsilon^*(B_8,\ell)\ge
\hat\epsilon^*(B_{12},\ell)\ge
\hat\epsilon^*(B_{16},\ell).
]

This monotonicity is a design invariant, not a discovery.
