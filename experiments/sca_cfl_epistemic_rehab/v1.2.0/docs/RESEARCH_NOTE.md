# Epistemic rehabilitation with conserved genealogy

This gate distinguishes repair of bytes from rehabilitation of claims/models. Integrity failures cannot be cured by favorable evidence; the object identity itself failed. Epistemic quarantine may be reconsidered through new evidence with explicit genealogy.

A root-level e-process prevents replicated copies from masquerading as independent corroboration. A second shadow gate evaluates prospective performance. Even both evidence gates do not restore authority: authority is a separate event.

Historical NOAA replay is deliberately non-confirmatory: fixed_logistic underperformed source_frozen in 2016 but had much lower mean Brier during 2017-2025. The conservative sequential e-process nevertheless failed to cross the threshold, so no automatic activation occurs.
