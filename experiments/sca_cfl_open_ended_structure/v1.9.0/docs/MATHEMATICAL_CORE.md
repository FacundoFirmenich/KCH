# Gate 010 mathematical core

Discovery data live on support x=z. Therefore feature-x and feature-z predictors receive identical sufficient statistics and produce identical prequential predictions. The observation map is non-injective on the structural hypothesis space.

A support-breaking intervention x!=z creates a point where the frozen predictors differ. Conditional on the discovery stage, the future likelihood ratio between two frozen predictive models is an e-process under the denominator model.

The certificate is restricted to the searched grammar and declared support. It does not identify a globally unique causal ontology. Probabilistic models remain CFL protoform +0.
